One primary call-to-action, rendered as a fully-rounded pill — terracotta (accent), graphite (dark), or outline.

```jsx
<CTAButton variant="accent" arrow>Сохранить</CTAButton>
<CTAButton variant="dark">Читать в Telegram</CTAButton>
<CTAButton variant="outline" size="sm">Подписаться</CTAButton>
```

Use terracotta for the single most important action; graphite and outline are secondary. `arrow` appends ↗. Pass `href` to render an anchor.
