The small uppercase kicker that sits above a section title or inside a card header.

```jsx
<Eyebrow number="02">Цвет</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow color="var(--accent-2)">Позиционирование</Eyebrow>
```

Terracotta by default; pass `color="var(--accent-2)"` for the muted sage in-card variant. Optional `number` prefix.
